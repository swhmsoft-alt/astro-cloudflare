Titanium's biocompatibility, osseointegration capability, and corrosion resistance make it the preferred material for medical implants and surgical instruments.

## Typical Components

- **Orthopedic Implants** — Hip stems, knee components, bone plates, screws
- **Spinal Implants** — Pedicle screws, rods, interbody cages
- **Dental Implants** — Abutments, fixtures, healing caps
- **Surgical Instruments** — Forceps, retractors, drill guides
- **Trauma Devices** — Intramedullary nails, external fixators

## Material Grades

| Grade | Application |
|---|---|
| Ti-6Al-4V ELI (Grade 23) | Orthopedic and spinal implants |
| CP-Ti Grade 1-4 | Dental implants and non-load-bearing applications |
| Ti-6Al-7Nb | Alternative to Ti-6Al-4V for nickel-sensitive patients |

## Key Requirements

- ISO 13485 quality management system
- ASTM F136 / ASTM F67 material standards
- Cleanroom manufacturing and packaging
- Full lot traceability with MTR documentation
