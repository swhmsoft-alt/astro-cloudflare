Titanium is the material of choice for aerospace structural components due to its exceptional strength-to-weight ratio, corrosion resistance, and fatigue performance at elevated temperatures.

## Typical Components

- **Bulkheads** — Primary load-bearing frames in the fuselage structure
- **Ribs & Spars** — Wing and empennage structural members
- **Wing Boxes** — Center wing box assemblies
- **Engine Pylons** — Mounting structures for engines
- **Landing Gear Components** — High-load structural members

## Material Grades

| Grade | Application |
|---|---|
| Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5) | General-purpose structural components |
| Ti-6Al-4V ELI (Grade 23) | Fracture-critical applications |
| Ti-6Al-2Sn-4Zr-2Mo | High-temperature sections (up to 540°C) |

## Key Requirements

- Strict traceability per AS9100D
- First Article Inspection (FAI) per AS9102
- NADCAP-approved special processes
- Material certifications per ASTM standards
