Titanium's exceptional corrosion resistance in aggressive chemical environments makes it ideal for industrial processing equipment exposed to acids, chlorides, and seawater.

## Typical Components

- **Heat Exchangers** — Tube sheets, baffles, pressure vessels
- **Pump Components** — Impellers, casings, shafts
- **Valve Components** — Balls, seats, stems, bodies
- **Marine Hardware** — Propeller shafts, seawater piping
- **Chemical Reactors** — Agitators, baffles, linings

## Material Grades

| Grade | Application |
|---|---|
| CP-Ti Grade 2 | General corrosion-resistant service |
| Grade 12 (Ti-0.3Mo-0.8Ni) | Improved crevice corrosion resistance |
| Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5) | High-strength structural components |

## Key Requirements

- Weldability for fabricated assemblies
- Corrosion allowance for long service life
- Pressure vessel code compliance (ASME Section VIII)
- NACE MR0175 for sour service (if applicable)
