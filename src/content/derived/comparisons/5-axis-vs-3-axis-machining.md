## Comparison
| Factor | 3-Axis | 5-Axis |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Setup Count | Multiple | Single |
| Geometry | Simple to moderate | Complex/contoured |
| Surface Finish | Good | Excellent |
| Setup Time | Higher | Lower |
| Machine Cost | Lower | 2-3x higher |
| Part Cost (simple) | Lower | Higher |
| Part Cost (complex) | Not possible | Feasible |
