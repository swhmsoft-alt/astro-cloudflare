## Quick Answer

Choose **anodizing** for thicker oxide layers (2-50 µm), cosmetic color coding, enhanced wear resistance, and improved corrosion protection in marine environments. Choose **passivation** for thin, clean oxide layers (<0.1 µm) that maintain dimensional accuracy, remove surface contaminants, and provide corrosion resistance for medical devices and cleanroom components.

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Property | Anodizing | Passivation |
|---|---|---|
| Process Type | Electrochemical | Chemical (acid bath) |
| Oxide Thickness | 2-50 µm | <0.1 µm (natural) |
| Color Options | Multiple (voltage-dependent) | Natural (no color change) |
| Dimensional Change | +50% of coating thickness | Negligible |
| Wear Resistance | Good (Type III: excellent) | None (same as base material) |
| Corrosion Protection | Excellent | Good (restores passive layer) |
| Electrical Insulation | Yes (dielectric) | No |
| Cost | $$ | $ |
| Typical Applications | Aerospace, marine, cosmetic | Medical, cleanroom, food |

## Process Comparison

### Anodizing
- Electrochemical process using sulfuric or chromic acid electrolyte
- Voltage-controlled oxide growth: 10-100V produces colors (gold, blue, purple, gray)
- Thicker coatings for Type III (hard anodizing)
- Requires masking of critical surfaces

### Passivation
- Chemical process using nitric or citric acid
- Removes free iron and surface contaminants
- Restores the natural passive oxide layer (1-5 nm)
- No dimensional change — safe for precision components

## Recommended Applications

### Choose Anodizing When
- Color coding for assembly identification
- Wear resistance needed (Type III hard anodizing)
- Marine corrosion protection
- Cosmetics matter (uniform appearance)
- Dielectric properties needed

### Choose Passivation When
- Medical device cleanliness (ASTM F86)
- Precision tolerances cannot accommodate coating thickness
- Surface chemistry matters more than appearance
- Regulatory requirement for medical devices
- Post-weld cleaning needed

## Selection Decision Framework

```
If color coding needed → Anodizing
If wear resistance critical → Anodizing (Type III)
If tight tolerances (±0.005mm) → Passivation
If medical implant → Passivation (per ASTM F86)
If marine environment → Anodizing
If electrical insulation needed → Anodizing
```
