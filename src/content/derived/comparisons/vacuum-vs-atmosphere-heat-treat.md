## Key Differences
| Factor | Vacuum | Atmosphere |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Alpha Case | None | 0.05-0.15 mm |
| Surface Finish | As-loaded | Oxidised |
| Cost Factor | 2-3x | 1x |
| Temperature Uniformity | ±5°C | ±10°C |
| Quench Rate | Slower (gas) | Faster (gas/liquid) |
