## Documents prove conformity
Buyers use certifications to verify that what was shipped matches what was specified. The required document depends on the standard and the buyer's quality system.

## KEEP THE GAP
Document requirements vary with standard and industry. Refer to the governing standard for what each certificate must contain. titanium.blog does not issue certifications.
