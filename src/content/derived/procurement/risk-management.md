## Risk lives in the contract
Most supply risk is manageable by writing clear acceptance, traceability, and liability terms before production starts.

## KEEP THE GAP
Risk exposure depends on the buyer's market, part criticality, and supplier base. titanium.blog offers neutral guidance, not risk advice for a specific supply chain.
