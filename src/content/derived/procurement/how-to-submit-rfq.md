## The submission loop
Send the full package in one go, then drive every open item to written confirmation. Suppliers price what they can see; incomplete RFQs lead to conditional quotes.

## What a good response includes
A complete response should state the scope, any assumptions or exclusions, the commercial terms, and which documents will be provided with delivery.

## KEEP THE GAP
titanium.blog does not operate an RFQ backend and is not a supplier. Submission formats and response practices vary by vendor; confirm with each supplier.
