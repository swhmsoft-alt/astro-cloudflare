## Quality systems set expectations
Aerospace procurement often carries specific quality and documentation expectations. Clarify which apply to your program rather than assuming.

## KEEP THE GAP
AS9100 and related requirements are defined by the SAE / IAQG. Refer to the official standard for normative text. titanium.blog is not a certification body.
