## Tubing Types
Seamless: 6-100mm OD ASTM B861 AMS 4945. Welded: 6-200mm OD ASTM B862. Capillary: 1-6mm OD.

## Grade Selection
Grade 2: chemical/marine. Grade 9: aerospace hydraulic. Grade 5: structural. Grade 7: corrosive chemical.
