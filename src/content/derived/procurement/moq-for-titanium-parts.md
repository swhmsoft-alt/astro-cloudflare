## MOQ is a negotiation variable
MOQ is not a fixed physical law; it reflects a supplier's setup, material, and scheduling economics. Understanding what drives it helps you negotiate.

## KEEP THE GAP
MOQ values are vendor-specific commercial terms. titanium.blog is a neutral knowledge hub and does not publish supplier MOQ figures.
