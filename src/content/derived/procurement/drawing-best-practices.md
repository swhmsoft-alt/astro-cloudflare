## Clear callouts reduce disputes
Ambiguous drawings are a leading source of disagreement about whether a part conforms. Explicit grade, tolerance, and finish callouts keep both sides aligned.

## Material reference
Titanium grades are defined by standards (for example ASTM B265 for sheet and plate, B348 for bar). Reference the applicable grade and condition.

## KEEP THE GAP
titanium.blog publishes neutral drawing guidance, not a drafting standard. Refer to the relevant standard document for normative requirements.
