## Why a checklist matters
An RFQ is a contract boundary for a quote. Omitted details such as material condition, tolerance, and certification are a common source of scope drift and later claims.

## What to confirm with the supplier
Ask which file formats are accepted, whether a formal drawing is required versus a 3D model alone, and which documents the supplier will ship with the parts.

## KEEP THE GAP
titanium.blog is a neutral knowledge hub, not a supplier. Actual lead times, minimums, and certification scopes are vendor-specific and must be confirmed with each supplier. We do not publish unverifiable commercial figures.
