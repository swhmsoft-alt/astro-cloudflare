## FAI locks the production baseline
FAI establishes that the production process, not a one-off, yields conforming parts. Scope it clearly to avoid ambiguity.

## KEEP THE GAP
FAI requirements follow the governing standard (for example AS9102) and the buyer's quality system. titanium.blog does not set inspection standards.
