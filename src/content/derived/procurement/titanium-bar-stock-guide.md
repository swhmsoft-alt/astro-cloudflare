## Standard Bar Forms
Round Bar: 6-400mm dia ASTM B348 AMS 4928. Hex Bar: 6-50mm A/F. Square Bar: 6-100mm. Flat Bar: 3-50mm x 12-200mm ASTM B265.

## Temper Options
Annealed standard. Solution Treated and Aged for Grade 5 high-strength. Stress Relieved.
