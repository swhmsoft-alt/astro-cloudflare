## Packaging is a documented deliverable
Under-specified packaging causes damage claims and customs delays. Define it in the contract, not by assumption.

## KEEP THE GAP
Packaging standards and export documentation requirements vary by route, destination, and commodity. titanium.blog does not prescribe shipping norms.
