## Terms are part of the contract
Payment structure interacts with delivery and Incoterms. Documenting the schedule protects both sides.

## KEEP THE GAP
Credit terms and deposits are supplier-specific and often credit-dependent. titanium.blog does not prescribe commercial terms.
