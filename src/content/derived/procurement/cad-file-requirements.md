## Format matters less than clarity
The important point is that the supplier can open the geometry and that units and tolerances are unambiguous. Confirm in writing before sending large files.

## Revision control
Agree on how model revisions are tracked so both sides quote and build against the same revision.

## KEEP THE GAP
Accepted formats and workflows are vendor-specific. titanium.blog does not prescribe a single format; confirm with each supplier.
