## Thickness Ranges
Sheet: 0.4-4.8mm Grades 1/2/5/9. Plate: 4.8-100mm Grades 2/5/23. Heavy Plate: 100-200mm Grades 5/23.

## Specifications
ASTM B265 all grades. AMS 4911 Grade 5 aerospace. AMS 4909 Grade 6.
