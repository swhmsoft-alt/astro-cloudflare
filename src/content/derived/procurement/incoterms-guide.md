## Incoterms remove ambiguity
Incoterms 2020 standardize the point of cost and risk transfer. Picking one and documenting it prevents disputes over freight and responsibility.

## KEEP THE GAP
Incoterm rules are defined by the ICC; refer to the official Incoterms 2020 publication for normative text. titanium.blog does not restate legal obligations as its own.
