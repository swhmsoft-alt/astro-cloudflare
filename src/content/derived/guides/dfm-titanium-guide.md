## 1. Avoid Deep Pockets
Limit to 4x tool diameter. Deeper need specialty tooling.

## 2. Realistic Tolerances
Standard ±0.025 mm. Precision ±0.005 mm. Ultra ±0.001 mm adds cost.

## 3. Standard Tooling
Corner radii min 1.5 mm. 3 mm when possible. Avoid custom tools.

## 4. Minimise Setups
Single orientation access. 5-axis ideal.

## 5. Material Utilisation
Titanium -150/kg. Nest parts efficiently. Near-net shapes.

## 6. Avoid Thin Walls
Min 0.5 mm small 1.0 mm structural. Thin walls cause vibration.
