## 1. ASTM F136 Compliance
Grade 23 must comply with ASTM F136 or ISO 5832-3.

## 2. ISO 13485
Supplier must operate under ISO 13485.

## 3. Surface Finish
Bearing surfaces Ra ≤ 0.1. Bone-contact Ra 0.5-2.0.

## 4. Cleanliness
Validated cleaning. Class 7 cleanroom packaging.

## 5. Post-Processing
DMLS implants need HIP and stress relief. Post-machine critical surfaces.
