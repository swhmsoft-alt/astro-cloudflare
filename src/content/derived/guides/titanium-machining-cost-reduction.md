## 1. Design Optimisation
Increase corner radii for standard tools. Relax tolerances. Combine features.

## 2. Material Optimisation
Near-net shapes forging DMLS. Grade 5 over 23 when ELI not needed.

## 3. Process Optimisation
High-feed milling for roughing. Trochoidal paths for deep slots.

## 4. Supplier Collaboration
Share volumes for better pricing. Provide 3D models. Allow alternative methods.
