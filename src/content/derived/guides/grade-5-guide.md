## 1. Properties
Grade 5: 950 MPa tensile at 4.43 g/cm³. 50% of global titanium.

## 2. Material Certification
ASTM B265 sheet ASTM B348 bar. Aerospace: AMS 4928 or AMS 4911.

## 3. Machining
Rough mill 150-250 SFM AlTiN carbide. Coolant 1000+ PSI.

## 4. Design
Min corner radius 0.5 mm. 5-axis access. Realistic tolerances.
