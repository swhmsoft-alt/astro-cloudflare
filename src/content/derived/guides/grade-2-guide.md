## 1. When to Specify
Corrosion resistance and formability primary. 345 MPa sufficient.

## 2. Certification
ASTM B265 sheet B348 bar B861 seamless pipe B862 welded.

## 3. Forming
Cold form 2-3x bend radius. Spring-back 2-3 degrees.

## 4. Welding
TIG with ERTi-2 filler. Argon 99.995% pure both sides.

## 5. Surface Finish
As-rolled anodizing for colour. Passivation for corrosion.
