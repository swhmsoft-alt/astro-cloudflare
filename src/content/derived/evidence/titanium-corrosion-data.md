## Corrosion Rates
| Environment | Temperature | Rate (mm/year) |
|-------------|-------------|----------------|
| Seawater | 25°C | <0.001 |
| 10% HCl (Grade 2) | 25°C | 0.5-1.5 |
| 10% HCl (Grade 7) | 25°C | <0.01 |
| 65% HNO₃| 60°C | <0.05 |
| Wet Chlorine | 25°C | <0.01 |

## Evidence Basis

Based on compiled corrosion data referenced to ASTM corrosion standards and handbooks. Rates are typical/indicative per environment, not single-standard-certified values — actual rates depend on temperature, aeration, and alloy condition.

## Engineering Interpretation (titanium.blog)

(titanium.blog) Titanium resists oxidizing media (seawater, wet chlorine, nitric acid) with corrosion rates below 0.05 mm/yr, but is attacked by hot reducing acids (HCl, H2SO4). Palladium-alloyed Grade 7 shifts reducing-acid rates down dramatically (<0.02 mm/yr). Treat these as indicative screening data for material selection, not a guarantee for a specific service.
