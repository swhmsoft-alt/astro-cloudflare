## Anodizing Voltage-Colour Relationship (Type II)
| Voltage | Colour | Oxide Thickness |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| 10V | Bronze/Gold | 200Å |
| 20V | Purple/Blue | 400Å |
| 30V | Light Blue | 600Å |
| 50V | Green | 1000Å |
| 70V | Pink/Magenta | 1400Å |
| 90V | Green-Yellow | 1800Å |
| 110V | Rose | 2200Å |
