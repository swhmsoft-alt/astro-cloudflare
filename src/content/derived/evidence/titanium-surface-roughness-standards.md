| Aerospace structural | 0.4-0.8 µm | Finish mill |

## Evidence Basis

Based on ASME B46.1 and ISO 1302, which define surface-texture measurement methodology and symbology — they do NOT prescribe per-application Ra values. The Ra-by-application figures below are titanium.blog's engineering recommendations, not standard requirements.

## Engineering Interpretation (titanium.blog)

(titanium.blog) ASME B46.1 / ISO 1302 standardize how Ra is measured and drawn, but selecting the target Ra for a given application is an engineering judgment. The values here are titanium.blog's typical recommendations (e.g., electropolished bearing surfaces to 0.05-0.1 µm, aerospace structural finish-milled to 0.4-0.8 µm). Verify against the end-customer drawing.
