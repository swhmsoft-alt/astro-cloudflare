## Surface Finish by Application
| Application | Ra Required | Process |
|-------------|-------------|---------|
| Medical implant bearing | 0.05-0.1 µm | Electropolish |
| Aerospace structural | 0.4-0.8 µm | Finish mill |
| Semiconductor UHV | 0.1-0.2 µm | Electropolish |
| Chemical processing | 0.8-1.6 µm | As-machined |
| Automotive cosmetic | 0.05-0.2 µm | Polish |
| Marine propeller | 0.8-3.2 µm | Machined/ground |
