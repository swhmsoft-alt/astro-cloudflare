## TLDR

ASTM B265 is the primary standard governing titanium and titanium alloy sheet, strip, and plate products. It covers dimensions, chemical composition, mechanical properties, and testing requirements for all commercially available titanium grades.

## Scope

ASTM B265 covers titanium and titanium alloy sheet, strip, and plate in the following conditions: annealed, cold-worked, and heat-treated. The standard defines thickness ranges from 0.4 mm to 100 mm.
