## Quick Answer
**What is polishing for titanium?** Mechanical polishing uses progressive abrasive grits to achieve mirror-like surfaces on titanium. Typical sequences start at 120-grit and progress through 240, 400, 600, 800, 1000, and up to 3000-grit for mirror finishes.
## Grit Sequence
| Stage | Grit | Resulting Ra |
|-------|------|-------------|
| Rough | 120–240 | 0.8 µm |
| Intermediate | 400–600 | 0.4 µm |
| Fine | 800–1200 | 0.2 µm |
| Mirror | 2000–4000+ | 0.05 µm |
