## Quick Answer
**What is electropolishing for titanium?** Electropolishing is an electrochemical process that removes surface material from titanium components, producing a mirror-like finish with Ra values down to 0.05 碌m. It also passivates the surface and removes the disturbed layer from previous machining operations.
## Applications
- Medical implant bearing surfaces (hip stems, knee components)
- Semiconductor UHV chamber components requiring minimal outgassing
- Aerospace components requiring improved fatigue life
