## Quick Answer
**What is bead blasting for titanium?** Bead blasting propels fine glass or ceramic beads at titanium surfaces under controlled pressure to create a uniform matte finish, remove light surface contamination, and induce beneficial compressive residual stresses.
## Parameters
| Media | Pressure | Resulting Ra |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Glass beads (60–100 mesh) | 2–4 bar | 0.8–1.6 µm |
| Ceramic beads (80–120 mesh) | 3–5 bar | 1.2–3.2 µm |
## Applications
- Uniform matte surface finish for medical implants
- Surface preparation before coating or bonding
- Removal of light oxidation from heat-treated titanium
