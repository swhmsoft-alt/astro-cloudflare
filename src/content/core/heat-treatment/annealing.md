## Quick Answer
**What is titanium annealing?**
Annealing at 700-850°C relieves stresses and improves ductility.

## By Grade
| Grade | Temp | Time | Cooling | Hardness |
|-------|------|------|---------|----------|

| Grade 5 | 730-845°C | 1-4 h | Air | 32-36 HRC |
