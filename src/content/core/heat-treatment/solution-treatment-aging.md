## Quick Answer
**What is STA?**
Solution treatment at 900-980°C + quench + aging at 480-595°C maximizes strength.

## Properties After STA
| Alloy | UTS (ksi) | YS (ksi) | Elong (%) | Hardness |
|-------|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|
| Ti-6Al-4V | 150-170 | 140-160 | 8-12 | 36-40 HRC |
| Ti-6Al-4V ELI | 140-160 | 130-150 | 10-15 | 34-38 HRC |
| Ti-10V-2Fe-3Al | 180-200 | 170-190 | 6-10 | 40-44 HRC |
