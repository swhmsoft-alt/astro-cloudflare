## Quick Answer
Stress relief at 480-650°C reduces residual stresses from machining.

## Parameters
| Grade | Temperature | Hold Time | Atmosphere |
|-------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| CP (1-4) | 480-595°C | 1-2 h | Vacuum/argon |
| Grade 5 | 540-650°C | 1-4 h | Vacuum/argon |
