## Quick Answer
Alpha case, hydrogen pickup, grain growth, and distortion are common defects.

## Defects & Prevention
| Defect | Cause | Prevention |
|--------|-------|------------|
| Alpha Case | Oxygen diffusion | Vacuum atmosphere |
| Hydrogen Pickup | Moisture | Vacuum < 10^-4 torr |
| Grain Growth | Above beta transus | Temperature control |
