## Quick Answer
**What is heat treatment for titanium?**
Heat treatment modifies mechanical properties through controlled heating and cooling.

## Common Cycles
| Treatment | Temperature | Duration | Purpose |
|-----------|-------------|----------|---------|
| Stress Relief | 480-650°C | 1-4 h | Reduce stresses |
| Annealing | 700-850°C | 1-6 h | Soften for forming |
| Solution Treat | 900-980°C | 1-2 h | Prepare for aging |
| Aging | 480-595°C | 4-24 h | Increase strength |
