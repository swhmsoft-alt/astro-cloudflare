## Quick Answer

**What is heat treatment for titanium?**  
Heat treatment for titanium alloys involves controlled heating and cooling cycles to modify mechanical properties, relieve residual stresses, or develop specific microstructures. Unlike steel, titanium undergoes no eutectoid transformation, so heat treatment primarily controls alpha-beta phase proportions and precipitate distribution.

- Stress relief of machined titanium components
