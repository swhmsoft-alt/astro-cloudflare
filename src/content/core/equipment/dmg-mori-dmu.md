## Quick Answer
**What is the DMG MORI DMU series?** DMG MORI DMU series 5-axis machining centres offer simultaneous 5-axis machining capability for complex titanium components. Available in various sizes from DMU 60 to DMU 200 with spindle speeds up to 28,000 rpm and torque up to 430 Nm.
## Key Capabilities
- Simultaneous 5-axis and 3+2 positioning for complex titanium geometries
- High-torque spindles (430 Nm) for aggressive titanium roughing
- Through-spindle coolant up to 80 bar for titanium chip evacuation
- MSC (milling spindle cooling) for thermal stability during long titanium cuts
