## Overview

The Heinz Blum TAF-65 is a high-precision automatic bar feeder enabling continuous unattended operation for cylindrical titanium parts.

## Key Specifications

- **Bar Diameter Range:** Ø3–65 mm
- **Maximum Bar Length:** 2,000 mm
- **Feeding Accuracy:** ±0.1 mm

## Typical Applications

- Medical screw and implant production from Ti-6Al-4V and commercial pure titanium
- Aerospace fasteners and fittings manufacturing
- Automotive hydraulic component families

## Engineering Notes

Automatic bar feeders reduce cycle time by 80% compared to manual loading and enable lights-out manufacturing for long production runs of cylindrical titanium parts. The precision positioning system ensures consistent part length tolerance across thousands of cycles.
