## Overview

The Coherent SurfaTech-500 is a comprehensive surface treatment line capable of multiple finishing processes for titanium components.

## Key Specifications

- **Process Types:** Type II + Type III anodizing, chemical passivation, optional PVD coating
- **Batch Capacity:** Up to 500 kg per cycle
- **Film Thickness Control:** 2–25 μm (anodized), 1–3 μm (PVD)

## Typical Applications

- Aerospace component corrosion protection with dielectric isolation
- Medical implant biocompatible oxide layer formation
- Decorative titanium finishes for high-end consumer electronics

## Engineering Notes

Titanium anodizing creates a controlled TiO₂ oxide layer where color is determined by interference effects based on thickness. Process parameters must be precisely controlled to meet AMS 5986 (aerospace) and ISO 5832-11 (medical) requirements.
