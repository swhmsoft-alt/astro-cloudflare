## Overview

The Mitsubishi EV3000 is a high-precision wire EDM machine using 5-axis simultaneous motion for complex titanium component fabrication.

## Key Specifications

- **Axes:** 5 simultaneous motion paths (X, Y, Z, U, V)
- **Maximum Part Size:** 600 × 400 × 300 mm
- **Positioning Tolerance:** ±2 μm

## Typical Applications

- Aerospace turbine blades with complex airfoil profiles
- Medical implant micro-features (porous structures, micro-channels)
- Extrusion die manufacturing for titanium tube production

## Engineering Notes

Wire EDM eliminates mechanical forces during cutting, making it ideal for thin-walled titanium components that would deform under conventional machining loads. The process achieves surface roughness Ra ≤ 0.2 μm without secondary finishing.
