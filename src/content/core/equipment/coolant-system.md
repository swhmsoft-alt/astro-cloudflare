## Overview

The EDP-Coolant HP-150 is a high-pressure through-spindle coolant system designed to deliver pressurized coolant directly to the cutting zone, improving chip evacuation and extending tool life.

## Key Specifications

- **Operating Pressure:** 100–150 bar (1,450–2,175 psi)
- **Maximum Flow Rate:** 150 L/min
- **Filtration Type:** Hinge-belt + drum filtration system

## Typical Applications

- Deep hole drilling in aerospace structural components
- High-feed milling operations on titanium alloys
- Titanium tube and hollow component manufacturing

## Engineering Notes

High-pressure coolant dramatically improves chip evacuation in titanium machining, reducing tool wear by up to 60% and enabling higher cutting speeds without sacrificing surface finish quality.
