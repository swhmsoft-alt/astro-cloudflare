## Overview

The Mitutoyo CRP-A507 is a bridge-type CMM providing traceable dimensional measurement for critical titanium aerospace and medical components.

## Key Specifications

- **Measuring Range:** 700 × 700 × 500 mm (adjustable up to 1,000 mm X-axis)
- **Absolute Accuracy:** ±2 μm over full working volume
- **Repeatability:** 1.2 μm (k=2, 95% confidence)

## Typical Applications

- First Article Inspection (FAI) per AS9102 standard
- Production part quality control for batch manufacturing
- Aerospace structural component verification (bulkheads, ribs, spars)

## Engineering Notes

CMMs enable complete geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) verification including true position, flatness, and profile tolerances required by MIL-STD-886 and ASME Y14.5 standards for aerospace parts.
