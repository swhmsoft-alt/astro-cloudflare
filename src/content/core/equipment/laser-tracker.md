## Overview

The Leica AT401 is a high-precision laser tracker providing portable coordinate measurement for large titanium structures and assemblies.

## Key Specifications

- **Measurement Radius:** 60–80 meters (line-of-sight)
- **Accuracy:** ±15 μm + 10 μm per meter distance
- **Data Acquisition Speed:** Up to 1,000 points/second

## Typical Applications

- Large aerospace structure assembly verification (wing sections, fuselage panels)
- Jig and fixture calibration for CNC machining operations
- Reverse engineering of legacy titanium components for redesign

## Engineering Notes

Laser trackers bridge the gap between CMM precision and portability, enabling on-site measurement of large titanium structures that cannot fit in traditional metrology equipment.
