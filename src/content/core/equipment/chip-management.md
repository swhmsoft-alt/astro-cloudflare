## Overview

The MagnaChip TC-500 is a comprehensive chip management system with integrated fire suppression for safe and efficient titanium machining operations.

## Key Specifications

- **Filtration Type:** Hinge-belt + drum filtration
- **Daily Chip Capacity:** Up to 2,000 kg/day
- **Fire Suppression:** Automatic nitrogen-based system

## Typical Applications

- High-volume titanium milling operations
- Continuous unattended production environments
- Aerospace component family manufacturing

## Engineering Notes

Titanium chips are pyrophoric (self-igniting at high temperatures). This system's continuous chip removal and nitrogen fire suppression eliminate explosion risks while achieving 95%+ chip recovery rates.
