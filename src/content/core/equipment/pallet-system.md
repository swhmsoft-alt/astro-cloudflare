## Overview

The ABB IRB-6700 is a high-payload industrial robot integrated into an automated pallet management system enabling fully unattended titanium machining operations.

## Key Specifications

- **Pallet Capacity:** 6–60 pallets per automated cell (configurable)
- **Cycle Time:** < 90 seconds per load/unload operation
- **Positioning Repeatability:** ±0.05 mm

## Typical Applications

- Multi-pallet family production for aerospace structural components
- Lights-out manufacturing cells operating 24/7 without operator intervention
- High-volume aerospace component families requiring consistent quality

## Engineering Notes

Robotic pallet systems increase equipment utilization from 30% (manual) to >85% by eliminating non-cut time. The system includes automatic part verification, chip removal, and tool change monitoring to ensure continuous production integrity.
