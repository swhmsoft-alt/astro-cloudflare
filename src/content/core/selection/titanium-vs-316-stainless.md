## Quick Answer
Titanium: 40% lighter, superior corrosion, 3-5x cost of 316 SS.

| Property | Grade 5 Ti | 316 SS |

| Density (lb/in3) | 0.16 | 0.29 |
| UTS (ksi) | 130 | 75 |
| Seawater | Excellent | Pitting risk |
| Cost | 4-5x | 1x |
