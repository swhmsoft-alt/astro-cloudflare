## Quick Answer
Inconel to 1300F. Ti limited to 600F. Ti better strength/weight below 600F.

## Comparison
| Property | Ti-6Al-4V | Inconel 718 |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| Max service (F) | 600 | 1300 |
| Density (lb/in3) | 0.16 | 0.30 |
| UTS at 70F (ksi) | 130 | 180 |
