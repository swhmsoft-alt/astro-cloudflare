## Quick Answer
Primary failure modes: hydrogen embrittlement, SCC, fatigue, ductile overload, creep.

## Failure Modes
| Mode | Frequency | Prevention |
|------|-----------|------------|
| Fatigue | High | Surface treatment |
| SCC | Moderate | Alloy selection |
| H-embrittlement | Moderate | Process control |
