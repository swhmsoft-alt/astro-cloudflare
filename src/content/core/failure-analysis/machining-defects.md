## Quick Answer
BUE, thermal damage, work hardening, chatter, burr formation.

## Common Issues
| Defect | Cause | Solution |
|--------|-------|----------|
| Built-up edge | Low speed | Increase speed, coated carbide |
| Thermal damage | Low coolant | High-pressure coolant > 1000 psi |
| Work hardening | Dull tool | Regular tool changes |
| Chatter | Low rigidity | Increase feed, reduce overhang |
