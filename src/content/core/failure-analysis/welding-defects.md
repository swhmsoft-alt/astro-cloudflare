## Quick Answer
Porosity, embrittlement, discoloration, cracking. Inert shielding required above 250°C.

## Weld Color Guide
| Color | Condition |
|-------|-----------|
| Silver | Excellent |
| Light straw | Acceptable |
| Dark blue | Reject |
| Grey/white | Severe contamination |
