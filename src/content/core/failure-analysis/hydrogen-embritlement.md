## Quick Answer
Hydrogen embrittlement forms brittle TiH2 hydrides. Sources: pickling, welding, cathodic charging.

## Critical Levels
| Grade | Safe (ppm) | Threshold |
|-------|------------|-----------|
| Grade 2 | < 150 | > 200 |
| Grade 5 | < 125 | > 150 |

## Prevention
- Vacuum furnace < 10^-4 torr
- Limit HF/HNO3 pickling time
