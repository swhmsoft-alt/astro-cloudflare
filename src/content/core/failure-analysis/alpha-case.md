## Quick Answer
Alpha case is an oxygen-enriched brittle surface layer from heat treatment above 480°C in air.

## Removal
| Method | Rate | Finish |
|--------|------|--------|
| Chemical milling | 0.001-0.003 in/min | 32-64 Ra |
| Machining | 0.002-0.010 in/pass | 16-32 Ra |
