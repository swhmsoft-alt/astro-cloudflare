## Quick Answer
**What are titanium's marine applications?** Titanium is virtually immune to seawater corrosion, offering unlimited service life in marine environments without coatings or cathodic protection.
## Applications
- Propeller shafts and propellers
- Seawater piping systems and heat exchangers
- Desalination plant components
- Subsea ROV and AUV housings
- Yacht fittings and rigging
