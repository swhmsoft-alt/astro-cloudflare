## Quick Answer
**What are titanium's automotive uses?** Titanium provides 40-60% weight reduction vs steel, high-temperature capability, and corrosion resistance for performance and luxury vehicles.
## Applications
- Exhaust systems (Grade 2, Grade 5)
- Connecting rods (Ti-10V-2Fe-3Al, Grade 5)
- Valve springs, suspension springs, driveshafts
