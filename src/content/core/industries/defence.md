## Quick Answer
**What are titanium's defence applications?** Titanium provides lightweight ballistic protection, high-strength structural components for military aircraft, and corrosion-resistant naval systems.
## Applications
- Armour plating for vehicles and aircraft
- Fighter jet structural components
- Naval propeller and shaft systems
- Ordnance and missile components
- Soldier equipment (helmet systems, exoskeleton frames)
