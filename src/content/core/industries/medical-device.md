## Quick Answer
**What is medical device manufacturing with titanium?** Production of implantable and non-implantable medical components under ISO 13485, FDA QSR, or EU MDR regulations. Titanium is preferred for biocompatibility and corrosion resistance.
## Applications
- Orthopaedic implants (hip stems, knee components, trauma plates, spinal cages)
- Dental implants and abutments
- Surgical instruments and cutting guides
- Custom patient-specific implants via DMLS
