## Quick Answer
**What is titanium used for in chemical processing?** Titanium offers exceptional corrosion resistance in chlorides, oxidising acids, and wet chlorine. Used for heat exchangers, reactors, piping, and vessels.
## Applications
- Heat exchangers and condensers for chloride service
- Reaction vessels for oxidising acid processes
- Piping systems for corrosive chemical transfer
- Chlorine dioxide bleaching equipment (pulp and paper)
