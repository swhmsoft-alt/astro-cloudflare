## Quick Answer
**What are titanium's applications in semiconductor manufacturing?** Titanium is used for its corrosion resistance, non-magnetic properties, and UHV compatibility with minimal outgassing.
## Applications
- Vacuum chamber walls and components
- Wafer handling end-effectors and grippers
- Showerheads and gas distribution plates
- Electrostatic chuck components
