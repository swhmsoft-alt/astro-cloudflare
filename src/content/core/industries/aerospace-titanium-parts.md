## TLDR

Titanium is essential in aerospace manufacturing, offering the highest strength-to-weight ratio of any structural metal.

## Applications

- Airframe Structures: Wing boxes, fuselage frames, empennage components
- Engine Parts: Fan blades, compressor discs, casings
- Landing Gear: Struts, torque links, actuator housings
- Fasteners: Bolts, screws, rivets for structural assembly

## Why Titanium for Aerospace?

- Highest strength-to-weight ratio of any structural metal
- Excellent fatigue resistance and corrosion resistance
- Compatible with carbon fiber composites
- Reliable performance at elevated temperatures (up to 400°C)

## Related Standards

- AS9100D — Aerospace quality management system
- NADCAP — Special process certification
- ASTM B265 — Sheet, strip, and plate
- ASTM B348 — Bars and billets
- AMS 4928 — Ti-6Al-4V bar and rod specification
