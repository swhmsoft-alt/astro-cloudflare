## Quick Answer
Titanium forms a stable TiO2 passive film providing exceptional corrosion resistance.

## Corrosion Rates
| Environment | Temp | Rate (mpy) |
|-------------|------|------------|
| Seawater | 25°C | < 0.1 |
| Nitric Acid 65% | 25°C | < 0.5 |
| HCl 5% | 25°C | 5-20 |
| H2SO4 10% | 25°C | 10-50 |
