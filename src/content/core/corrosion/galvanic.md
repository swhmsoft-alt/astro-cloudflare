## Quick Answer
Titanium is cathodic. Aluminum/steel coupled to titanium may corrode faster.

## Compatibility
| Metal | Risk | Action |
|-------|------|--------|
| 316 SS | Low | Safe |
| Aluminum | Moderate | Insulate |
| Carbon Steel | High | Coat |
