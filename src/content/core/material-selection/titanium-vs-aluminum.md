## Quick Answer
Ti: higher temp (600 vs 350F), 60% denser. Al: lower cost.

## Strength-to-Weight
| Property | Ti-6Al-4V | 6061-T6 | 7075-T6 |
|----------|-----------|---------|---------|
| Density (lb/in3) | 0.16 | 0.098 | 0.101 |
| UTS/weight | 812 | 450 | 820 |
| Max temp (F) | 600 | 350 | 250 |
