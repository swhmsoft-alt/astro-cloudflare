## Quick Answer
Ti-6Al-4V accounts for 50% of aerospace Ti usage.

## Selection
| Component | Alloy | Requirement |
|-----------|-------|-------------|
| Airframe | Ti-6Al-4V | Strength/weight |
| Landing gear | Ti-10V-2Fe-3Al | 200 ksi |
| Engine disc | Ti-6242 | Creep to 1000F |
