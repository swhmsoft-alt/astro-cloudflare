## Quick Answer

**What is wire EDM for titanium?**  
Wire electrical discharge machining (EDM) uses a thin electrically charged wire to erode titanium through controlled spark discharges. It is ideal for producing precise contours, sharp internal corners, and burr-free features in hardened titanium components without applying cutting forces.
