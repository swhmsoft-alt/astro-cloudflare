## Quick Answer

**What is waterjet cutting for titanium?**  
Abrasive waterjet cutting uses a high-pressure stream of water mixed with garnet abrasive to erode titanium without generating heat. This cold-cutting process eliminates heat-affected zones, making it ideal for titanium components where metallurgical integrity must be preserved.
