## Quick Answer
Grade 2 cold formable. Grade 5 requires hot forming at 540-815°C. Springback 2-3x steel.

## Forming Limits
| Grade | Min Bend Radius | Temp |
|-------|----------------|------|
| Grade 2 | 2t | Ambient-200°C |
| Grade 5 annealed | 4t | 540-650°C |
| Grade 5 STA | 6t | 650-815°C |
