## Quick Answer
Titanium forged at 850-980°C (alpha-beta) or above 1000°C (beta).

## Forging Temps
| Alloy | Alpha-Beta | Beta |
|-------|------------|------|
| Ti-6Al-4V | 900-955°C | 1000-1050°C |
| Ti-6242 | 925-970°C | 1010-1060°C |
| Ti-10V-2Fe-3Al | 800-870°C | 900-950°C |
