## Quick Answer
Titanium requires inert gas shielding above 250°C. GTAW is most common. Laser and EBW offer deeper penetration.

## Process Comparison
| Process | Penetration | Speed | Cost |
|---------|-------------|-------|------|
| GTAW | 0.125 in | 6 ipm | Low |
| Laser | 0.250 in | 60 ipm | Medium |
| EBW | 2.000 in | 120 ipm | High |
