## Quick Answer

**What is heat treatment for titanium?**  
Heat treatment for titanium alloys involves controlled heating and cooling cycles to modify mechanical properties, relieve residual stresses, or develop specific microstructures. Unlike steel, titanium undergoes no eutectoid transformation, so heat treatment primarily controls alpha-beta phase proportions and precipitate distribution.

## Common Heat Treatment Cycles

| Treatment | Temperature | Duration | Cooling | Purpose |
|-----------|-------------|----------|---------|---------|
| Stress Relief | 480–650°C | 1–4 hours | Air cool | Reduce machining stresses |
| Annealing | 700–850°C | 1–6 hours | Furnace or air cool | Soften for forming |
| Solution Treat | 900–980°C | 1–2 hours | Water quench | Prepare for aging |
| Aging | 480–595°C | 4–24 hours | Air cool | Increase strength |

## Applications

- Stress relief of machined titanium components
- Annealing CP titanium for improved formability
- Solution treatment and aging of Ti-10V-2Fe-3Al for maximum strength
- Beta annealing of Ti-6242 for improved creep resistance
