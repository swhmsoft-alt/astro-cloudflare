## Quick Answer

**What is CNC milling for titanium?**  
CNC milling uses rotating multi-point cutting tools to remove material from titanium workpieces. It is the most versatile machining process for producing complex prismatic features including pockets, slots, contours, and threaded holes in titanium components.
