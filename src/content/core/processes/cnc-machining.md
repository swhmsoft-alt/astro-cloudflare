## TLDR

CNC machining is the most versatile and widely used manufacturing process for titanium components. Modern 5-axis CNC machines achieve tight tolerances of ±0.001 mm on complex titanium parts for aerospace, medical, and industrial applications.

## Quick Answer

**What is titanium CNC machining?**  
CNC (Computer Numerical Control) machining of titanium uses automated cutting tools to remove material from titanium stock, creating precise parts per engineering drawings. It is the preferred process for complex geometries requiring tight tolerances.
