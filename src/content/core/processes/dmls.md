## Quick Answer

**What is DMLS for titanium?**  
Direct Metal Laser Sintering (DMLS) is an additive manufacturing process that builds titanium components layer by layer from metal powder using a high-power laser. It enables the production of complex geometries, internal cooling channels, and lightweight lattice structures that cannot be manufactured through conventional subtractive methods.
