## Quick Answer

**What is CNC turning for titanium?**  
CNC turning is a machining process where titanium stock rotates against stationary cutting tools to produce cylindrical components. It is the preferred process for shafts, bushings, fittings, fasteners, and other round titanium parts requiring concentricity and tight diametral tolerances.

## Capability

CNC turning provides efficient production of cylindrical titanium components. Key capabilities include:

- **Single-setup machining** 鈥?turning, facing, boring, threading, and grooving in one operation
- **Live tooling** 鈥?milling, drilling, and tapping on rotated axes (for mill-turn centres)
- **Bar feed capability** 鈥?automated production from bar stock up to 100 mm diameter
- **Multi-axis turning** 鈥?subspindle for back-side operations, Y-axis for off-centre features
- **Minimum feature size** 鈥?0.5 mm (turning), 0.3 mm (grooving)
- **Maximum workpiece weight** 鈥?up to 500 kg
