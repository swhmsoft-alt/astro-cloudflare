## Quick Answer

**What is Grade 11 titanium?**  
Grade 11 is the low-oxygen version of Grade 7, with the same palladium content (0.12–0.25%) but lower oxygen (≤ 0.18%) to improve ductility and formability. It offers mechanical properties similar to Grade 1 with the enhanced corrosion resistance of the palladium alloying addition.

## Mechanical Properties

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Density | 4.51 g/cm³ |
| Tensile Strength | 240 MPa (min) |
| Yield Strength | 170 MPa (min) |
| Elongation | 24% |
| Hardness | 20 HRC |

## Applications

- Chemical processing equipment in reducing acid service requiring maximum formability
- Heat exchanger tubing for aggressive chemical environments
- Chlorine dioxide bleaching equipment in pulp and paper
- Seawater piping where both corrosion resistance and formability are critical
