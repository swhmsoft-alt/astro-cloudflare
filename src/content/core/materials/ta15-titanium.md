## Quick Answer

**What is TA15 titanium?**  
TA15 (Ti-6.5Al-2Zr-1Mo-1V) is a near-alpha titanium alloy developed for high-strength welded aerospace structures. It offers excellent thermal stability up to 500°C, superior weldability compared to Ti-6Al-4V, and good creep resistance. TA15 is widely specified for Chinese aerospace programs including airframe structural components, engine ducts, and rocket motor casings.
