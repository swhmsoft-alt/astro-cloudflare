## Quick Answer

**What is Grade 7 titanium?**  
Grade 7 (Ti-0.15Pd) is a CP titanium grade with a small palladium addition (0.12–0.25%) that dramatically improves corrosion resistance in reducing acid environments. It offers the same mechanical properties as Grade 2 but with superior resistance to crevice corrosion in hot chloride solutions, making it the preferred material for chemical processing equipment handling aggressive media.

## Chemical Composition

| Element | Weight % |
|---------|----------|
| Titanium (Ti) | balance |
| Palladium (Pd) | 0.12–0.25 |
| Iron (Fe) | ≤ 0.30 |
| Oxygen (O) | ≤ 0.25 |
| Carbon (C) | ≤ 0.08 |

## Applications

- **Chemical Processing**: Heat exchangers in HCl service, reaction vessels, piping for reducing acids, column internals
- **Oil & Gas**: Sour gas service components, produced water handling equipment
- **Marine**: Seawater heat exchangers, desalination plant components
- **Energy**: Geothermal well casings, flue gas desulfurisation scrubbers
