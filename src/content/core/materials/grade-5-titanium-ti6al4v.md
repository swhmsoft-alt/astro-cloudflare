## TLDR

Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5) is the workhorse titanium alloy, accounting for approximately 50% of all titanium used globally. It offers an excellent strength-to-weight ratio, corrosion resistance, and biocompatibility, making it the default choice for aerospace structures, medical implants, and high-performance industrial components.

## Quick Answer

**What is Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5) titanium?**  
Ti-6Al-4V is an alpha-beta titanium alloy containing 6% aluminum and 4% vanadium. It is the most commonly specified titanium alloy for CNC machining, offering a balanced combination of strength, ductility, and corrosion resistance.

## FAQ

**Is Grade 5 titanium difficult to machine?**  
Grade 5 is more challenging than steel or aluminum but readily machinable with proper tooling and parameters. Use sharp carbide tools, rigid setups, and adequate coolant.

**What is the difference between Grade 5 and Grade 23 titanium?**  
Grade 23 (Ti-6Al-4V ELI) is an extra-low interstitial version with improved fracture toughness, primarily used for medical implants requiring enhanced fatigue life.

**Can Grade 5 titanium be welded?**  
Yes, Ti-6Al-4V is weldable using TIG, MIG, and laser welding processes. Proper shielding gas and pre-weld cleaning are essential.
