## Quick Answer

**What is Grade 3 titanium?**  
Grade 3 is a medium-strength commercially pure titanium grade containing 99.1% minimum titanium. It occupies the middle ground between the highly formable Grade 1/2 grades and the higher-strength Grade 4. Grade 3 offers a balanced combination of corrosion resistance, moderate strength, and reasonable formability, making it suitable for chemical processing equipment, marine components, and industrial applications where higher strength than Grade 2 is required without the cost of alloy grades.

## Applications

- **Chemical Processing**: Heat exchangers, pressure vessels, pump housings, valve bodies, column internals
- **Marine**: Propeller shafts, seawater piping, offshore platform components, subsea equipment
- **Industrial**: Electrodes for electrowinning, anodes, bleaching equipment in pulp and paper
- **Energy**: Geothermal well components, condenser tubing, heat recovery systems
- **Aerospace**: Non-structural airframe components, ducting, clamps
