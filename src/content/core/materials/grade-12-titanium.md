## Quick Answer

**What is Grade 12 titanium?**  
Grade 12 (Ti-0.3Mo-0.8Ni) is a medium-strength titanium alloy with molybdenum and nickel additions that provide enhanced corrosion resistance, particularly in reducing acid and hot chloride environments. It offers strength between CP grades and alpha-beta alloys while maintaining excellent weldability and fabricability.

## Applications

- Chemical processing: Heat exchangers, pressure vessels for reducing acid service
- Oil & gas: Sour gas handling, produced water treatment
- Marine: Seawater heat exchangers, desalination equipment
- Energy: Geothermal brine handling, flue gas desulfurisation
