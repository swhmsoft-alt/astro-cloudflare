## Quick Answer

**What is Ti-10V-2Fe-3Al?**  
Ti-10V-2Fe-3Al is a beta-rich titanium alloy developed for high-strength aerospace forgings. It achieves tensile strengths exceeding 1240 MPa through heat treatment while offering excellent fracture toughness and fatigue resistance. Its superior forgeability compared to alpha-beta alloys allows complex shapes to be produced with fewer forming steps.
