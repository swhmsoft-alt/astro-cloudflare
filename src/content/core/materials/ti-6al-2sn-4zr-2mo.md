## Quick Answer

**What is Ti-6242 (Ti-6Al-2Sn-4Zr-2Mo)?**  
Ti-6242 is a near-alpha titanium alloy designed for high-temperature service up to 540°C. It offers excellent creep resistance, good tensile strength, and thermal stability, making it the material of choice for jet engine compressor discs, blades, and airframe components in high-temperature zones where standard titanium alloys like Ti-6Al-4V cannot operate.

## Applications

- Jet engine compressor discs, blades, and vanes
- High-temperature airframe structures
- Rocket engine components
- Power generation turbine components
- Defence propulsion systems
