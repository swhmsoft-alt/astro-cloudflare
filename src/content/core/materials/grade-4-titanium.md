## Quick Answer

**What is Grade 4 titanium?**  
Grade 4 is the highest-strength commercially pure titanium grade, with 99.0% minimum titanium content and the highest oxygen content (≤ 0.40%) among CP grades. It offers strength approaching that of alloyed titanium grades while retaining the excellent corrosion resistance and biocompatibility characteristic of CP titanium. Grade 4 is widely used in medical devices, chemical processing, and aerospace where moderate strength and full corrosion resistance are required.

## Applications

- **Medical**: Orthopaedic external fixation devices, surgical instruments, dental implants (non-load-bearing)
- **Chemical Processing**: High-strength vessel internals, pump shafts, valve components for corrosive media
- **Aerospace**: Structural brackets, fasteners, ducting supports
- **Industrial**: Fasteners, shafts, springs, structural components requiring corrosion resistance
