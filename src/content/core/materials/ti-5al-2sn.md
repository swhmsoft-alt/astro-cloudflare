## Quick Answer

**What is Ti-5Al-2.5Sn (Grade 6)?**  
Ti-5Al-2.5Sn is an alpha titanium alloy stabilised with aluminium and tin. It offers good strength at elevated temperatures (up to 480°C) and excellent cryogenic properties. Its fully alpha microstructure provides excellent weldability and corrosion resistance, making it suitable for both cryogenic pressure vessels and high-temperature airframe components.
