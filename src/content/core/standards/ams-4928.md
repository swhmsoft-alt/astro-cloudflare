## Quick Answer

**What is AMS 4928?**  
AMS 4928 is the SAE Aerospace Material Specification covering titanium alloy bars, wire, and forgings in Ti-6Al-4V. It requires tensile strength of 895 MPa minimum, yield of 830 MPa minimum, and elongation of 10% minimum with ultrasonic quality Grade AA.
