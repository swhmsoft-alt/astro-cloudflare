The Grade 23 titanium workpiece required 0.3mm thin webs between 1.2mm diameter locating holes. Tool deflection and thermal growth were causing consistent 0.012-0.015 mm positional drift."

result: "Positional accuracy improved from 0.015mm to 0.004mm (within spec). Thin web yield increased from 55% to 96%. Tool life improved from 12 to 38 parts per tool. Certification per ASTM F136 maintained. Reduced rework cost by $12,000 annually."
