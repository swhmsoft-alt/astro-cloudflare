## Quick Answer

Choose **Ti-6Al-4V** for applications where weight reduction (46% lighter), maximum corrosion resistance in seawater/chemical environments, and high strength-to-weight ratio are critical. Choose **316L stainless steel** where lower material cost (1/4 the price), higher stiffness (200 vs 114 GPa), and wider availability are priorities.

## Recommended Applications

### Choose Ti-6Al-4V When
- Weight reduction is critical (aerospace, racing, portable medical)
- Maximum corrosion resistance (seawater, chemical processing)
- High strength-to-weight ratio needed
- Biocompatibility required (medical implants)
- Service temperature up to 400°C

### Choose 316L SS When
- Cost is the primary constraint
- High stiffness required (long shafts, structural)
- Magnetic compatibility needed (austenitic = non-magnetic)
- Food/pharmaceutical processing equipment
- Wide availability in standard stock sizes

## Selection Decision Framework

```
If weight reduction critical → Ti-6Al-4V
If budget-constrained → 316L SS
If seawater immersion (long-term) → Ti-6Al-4V
If high stiffness required → 316L SS
If medical implant → Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 23)
If food processing equipment → 316L SS
If elevated temperature > 400°C → 316L SS
```
