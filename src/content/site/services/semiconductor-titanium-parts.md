## Overview

Titanium is an ideal material for semiconductor manufacturing equipment due to its corrosion resistance, non-magnetic properties, and compatibility with vacuum environments. We manufacture precision titanium components for semiconductor OEMs.

## Applications

- Vacuum chamber components
- Wafer handling and transport parts
- Process gas distribution components
- Etching and deposition system hardware
- Metrology fixture components

## Why Titanium for Semiconductor?

- Excellent corrosion resistance to process gases
- Non-magnetic — no interference with sensitive processes
- Low outgassing — suitable for UHV environments
- High strength-to-weight ratio
- Compatible with cleanroom requirements

## Quality Requirements

- Ultra-pure surface preparation
- Vacuum-compatible surface finishes
- Precision cleaning and packaging
- Material certifications and traceability
