## Overview

Titanium additive manufacturing (AM) using Selective Laser Melting (SLM) and Direct Metal Laser Sintering (DMLS) enables complex geometries impossible with traditional machining. Ideal for lightweight structures, custom implants, and rapid prototyping.

## Capabilities

| Specification | Capability |
|---|---|
| Build Volume | Up to 250 x 250 x 300 mm |
| Layer Thickness | 30-60 µm |
| Tolerance | ±0.1 mm (as-built) |
| Surface Finish | 6-10 µm Ra (as-built) |
| Lead Time | 5-10 business days (prototype) |

## Materials

- Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5) — Structural applications
- Ti-6Al-4V ELI (Grade 23) — Medical implants

## Applications

- Lightweight aerospace brackets and manifolds
- Custom medical implants and surgical guides
- Complex cooling channels and conformal features
- Rapid prototypes for functional testing
- Lattice structures for weight reduction

## Post-Processing Options

- Hot Isostatic Pressing (HIP)
- Stress relief and solution heat treat
- CNC machining of critical surfaces
- Surface finishing: polishing, anodizing, passivation
