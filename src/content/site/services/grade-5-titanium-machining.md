## Overview

Grade 5 titanium (Ti-6Al-4V) is the most widely used titanium alloy, accounting for approximately 50% of global titanium consumption. Our specialized machining processes are optimized for this alloy's unique properties.

## Material Properties

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Density | 4.43 g/cm³ |
| Tensile Strength | 950 MPa |
| Yield Strength | 880 MPa |
| Hardness | 36 HRC |
| Modulus of Elasticity | 114 GPa |
| Max Service Temp | 400°C |

## Applications

- Aerospace: Airframe structures, landing gear, fasteners
- Medical: Orthopedic implants, surgical instruments
- Industrial: High-performance components
- Automotive: Connecting rods, valves, exhaust
