## Overview

Titanium is the gold standard for medical implants and surgical instruments due to its biocompatibility, corrosion resistance, and osseointegration properties. Our medical manufacturing capabilities are fully ISO 13485 certified.

### capabilities

- **Orthopedic Implants**: Hip stems, knee components, spinal implants, and bone plates
- **Surgical Instruments**: Forceps, scissors, retractors, and custom surgical tooling
- **Dental Components**: Abutments, implants, and prosthetic frameworks
- **Medical Device Housings**: Pacemaker cases, monitoring device enclosures

### Quality Standards

- ISO 13485:2016 certified
- Cleanroom manufacturing available
- Clinical-grade surface finishes
- Full lot traceability and validation

### Materials

- Ti-6Al-4V ELI (Grade 23) — implant-grade
- CP Titanium (Grade 1-4) — surgical instruments
- Ti-6Al-7Nb — alternative implant alloy
