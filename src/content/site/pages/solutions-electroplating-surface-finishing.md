## Overview

Titanium is the preferred material for electroplating and surface finishing tooling due to its excellent corrosion resistance in acid and alkaline baths, long service life, and non-contaminating properties.

### capabilities

- **Anode Baskets**: Titanium baskets for precious metal plating
- **Jigs & Fixtures**: Custom plating racks and component holders
- **Heating & Cooling Coils**: Titanium immersion coils for bath temperature control
- **Tank Hardware**: Agitators, mixers, and support structures

### Key Advantages

- Chemically inert in most plating solutions
- No metallic contamination of bath chemistry
- Long service life — outlasts stainless steel 5:1
- Lightweight — easier handling and maintenance
