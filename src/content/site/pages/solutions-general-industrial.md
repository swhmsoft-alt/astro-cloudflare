## Overview

Beyond the well-known industries, titanium's unique properties solve engineering challenges across a vast range of industrial applications. Our team brings deep expertise to every custom project.

### Our Capabilities

- **Machinery Components**: Wear parts, bushings, and specialized tooling
- **Custom Fabrications**: Welded assemblies, brackets, and structural members
- **Laboratory Equipment**: Analytical instrument components and fixtures
- **Automation Parts**: Robotic end-effectors, grippers, and positioning components
- **Prototype & Low Volume**: Rapid-turnaround custom parts for R&D

### Why Titanium for Industrial Applications?

- Weight reduction in moving machinery components
- Corrosion resistance in harsh environments
- Non-magnetic for sensitive equipment
- High strength at elevated temperatures
- Excellent fatigue performance
