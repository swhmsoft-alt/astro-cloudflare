## Overview

Grade 23 titanium (Ti-6Al-4V ELI) is the extra-low interstitial version of Grade 5, offering improved fracture toughness and biocompatibility. It is the preferred material for medical implant applications and extreme low-temperature environments.

## Material Properties vs. Grade 5

| Property | Grade 23 (Ti-6Al-4V ELI) | Grade 5 (Ti-6Al-4V) |
|---|---|---|
| Oxygen Content | 0.13% max | 0.20% max |
| Tensile Strength | 860 MPa | 950 MPa |
| Fracture Toughness | Higher | Standard |
| Biocompatibility | ASTM F136 | ASTM B348 |

## Applications

- Medical implants: Hip stems, knee trays, spinal implants
- Surgical instruments
- Low-temperature aerospace components
- High-performance marine applications

## Applicable Standards

- ASTM F136 — Wrought Titanium-6Al-4V ELI for Medical Implants
- ISO 5832-3 — Implants for surgery
- ASTM B348 — Bars and billets
