## Overview

We manufacture precision titanium components for the aerospace industry under AS9100D quality systems. Our capabilities span structural airframe parts, engine components, and landing gear assemblies.

## Aerospace Certifications

- AS9100D — Quality Management System
- NADCAP — Chemical Processing (anodizing, passivation)
- Material certifications per ASTM B265, ASTM B348
- First Article Inspection (FAI) per AS9102

## Typical Components

- Structural brackets and fittings
- Engine mounts and housings
- Landing gear components
- Fasteners and hardware
- Hydraulic system components
- Interior hardware

## Materials

- Ti-6Al-4V (Grade 5) — Primary structural alloy
- Ti-6Al-4V ELI (Grade 23) — Fracture-critical applications
- Ti-6Al-2Sn-4Zr-2Mo — High-temperature applications
