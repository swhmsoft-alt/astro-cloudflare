## Overview

Our 5-axis titanium machining services enable complex geometries and tight tolerances that are impossible on traditional 3-axis machines. Simultaneous 5-axis machining reduces setups, improves surface finish, and delivers higher accuracy for critical components.

## Capabilities

| Specification | Capability |
|---|---|
| Axes | Simultaneous 5-axis (full contouring) |
| Tolerance | ±0.001 mm |
| Surface Finish | 0.2 µm Ra (standard) |
| Max Part Envelope | 800 mm diameter x 500 mm height |
| Spindle Speed | 12,000 - 20,000 RPM |
| Lead Time | 2-4 weeks (standard) |

## Why 5-Axis for Titanium?

- **Reduced setups**: Machine complex parts in one operation
- **Better tool access**: Reach difficult angles and deep cavities
- **Improved surface finish**: Continuous tool engagement
- **Tighter tolerances**: Fewer repositioning errors
- **Complex geometries**: Undercuts, draft angles, organic shapes

## Applications

- Aerospace turbine blades and impellers
- Medical implants with complex contours
- Semiconductor chamber components
- Robotics joints and structural frames
- Custom prototype geometries
