## DFM Guidelines for Titanium CNC Machining

- Maintain wall thickness above 0.5 mm
- Avoid sharp internal corners — use radii of 0.5 mm or larger
- Deep pockets (>3x depth) may require special tooling
- Threaded holes: specify thread depth and class of fit
- Include surface finish requirements on your drawing
