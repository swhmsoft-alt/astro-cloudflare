## Overview

Wire EDM (Electrical Discharge Machining) is ideal for titanium components requiring burr-free edges, tight tolerances, and complex shapes that are difficult to achieve with conventional cutting tools.

## Capabilities

| Specification | Capability |
|---|---|
| Max Thickness | 300 mm |
| Tolerance | ±0.002 mm |
| Surface Finish | 0.4 µm Ra |
| Corner Radius | From 0.05 mm |
| Lead Time | 1-3 weeks |

## Advantages for Titanium

- No tool pressure — ideal for thin walls and delicate features
- Burr-free edges — no secondary deburring required
- Excellent surface finish
- No heat-affected zone
- Can machine hardened titanium

## Applications

- Medical implant features and pockets
- Aerospace component details
- Precision tooling and fixtures
- Prototype and low-volume production
